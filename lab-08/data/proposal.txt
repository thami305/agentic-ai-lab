[passage PROP-P1 | section: Pricing]

Cloudline Systems proposes an annual platform fee of $48,000 for the Acme Corp
deployment, covering up to 10,000 active seats and standard onboarding. Fees
are payable quarterly in advance, with the first installment due on the
effective date. The initial term is thirty-six months from the effective date.
The vendor may increase the annual fee at each renewal anniversary at its
sole discretion, with no cap, index, or notice-period limitation. All fees are
exclusive of applicable taxes, which the customer is responsible for paying.

[passage PROP-P2 | section: Pricing]

Usage above the included 10,000 seats is billed as overage at $6 per seat per
month, metered monthly and invoiced in arrears. Payment terms are net
forty-five days from invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at one and
one half percent per month until paid in full. Professional services, if
required, are billed separately at $220 per hour with a forty-hour monthly
minimum commitment, and travel expenses are passed through at cost.

[passage PROP-P3 | section: Pricing]

If the customer terminates the agreement for convenience before the end of
the then-current term, an early termination fee equal to fifty percent of the
fees remaining under the then-current term is due within thirty days of the
termination notice. The early termination fee is in addition to any fees
already accrued and is not subject to proration. Termination for convenience
requires at least sixty days' prior written notice to the vendor.

[passage PROP-P4 | section: Terms]

This agreement will automatically renew for successive twelve-month terms
unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety
days before the end of the then-current term. Pricing for each renewal term
will reflect the fee schedule in effect at the time of renewal, including any
increases applied under the escalation provision. Renewal terms are otherwise
on the same conditions as the initial term unless the parties agree otherwise
in a signed amendment.

[passage PROP-P5 | section: Terms]

Except as prohibited by applicable law, neither party's total aggregate
liability under this agreement is capped. The vendor disclaims all implied
warranties to the fullest extent permitted by law. Neither party will be
liable for indirect or consequential damages, regardless of whether such
damages were foreseeable. Each party agrees to indemnify the other against
third-party claims arising from its own gross negligence or willful
misconduct.

[passage PROP-P6 | section: Terms]

Either party may terminate for cause if the other party materially breaches
this agreement and fails to cure within thirty days of written notice. The
vendor may suspend services on ten days' written notice if undisputed fees
remain unpaid for more than sixty days. The vendor may assign this agreement
without the customer's consent in connection with a merger, acquisition, or
sale of substantially all of its assets.

[passage PROP-P7 | section: Delivery/SLA]

The vendor will use its commercially reasonable best efforts to maintain
99.5% monthly platform uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance windows of up
to four hours per month. Service credits, if any, are at the vendor's sole
discretion and are the customer's exclusive remedy for any failure to meet
the uptime target. Uptime is measured by the vendor's own monitoring systems,
and the customer must report suspected outages within five business days to
be eligible for any consideration.

[passage PROP-P8 | section: Delivery/SLA]

Standard support is available during business hours, 8am to 6pm Pacific,
Monday through Friday, excluding vendor holidays. The vendor targets a
four-hour response time for priority-one incidents and a next-business-day
response for all other issues. The proposal states no service credit or other
remedy if these response targets are missed. Emergency after-hours support is
available for priority-one incidents only and is billed at time and materials.

[passage PROP-P9 | section: Terms]

This proposal is governed by the laws of the State of Washington. All
disputes will be resolved by binding arbitration in Seattle under the rules
of the American Arbitration Association. This proposal expires thirty days
after issuance and does not constitute a binding agreement until countersigned
by both parties. Notices under this agreement must be sent by email with
confirmed receipt or by overnight courier.
